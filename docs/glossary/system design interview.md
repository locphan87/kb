## Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                | Interviewers wants to see                            | What to do                                                            |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fail to demonstrate depth of knowledge | The scope of your knowledge                          | Reference **other** designs, solutions and case studies               |
| Not responding well to feedback        | Adaptability and eagerness to learn                  | Embrace any feedback or questions                                     |
| Fail to address design flaws           | Don't mind seeing flaws                              | Should not dismiss or defend them                                     |
| Designing off of assumptions           | Take action                                          | Ask questions to ensure you are not missing any important information |
| Not communicating your thought process | A glimpse into your design process and how you think | Communicate your ideas and discuss trade-offs                         |
